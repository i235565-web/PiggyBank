    How it works

    1. setGoal(goal) saves your target amount (write)
    2. deposit() is payable, it adds the ETH you send to your balance (write)
    3. withdraw() sends everything back to you, but only when balance is at least the goal (write)
    4. getSavings(address) returns balance, goal and number of deposits (read)
    5. goalReached(address) tells if you can withdraw yet (read)
